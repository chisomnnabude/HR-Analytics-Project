# HR-Analytics-Project
This project follows the HR Analytics Project provided as part of the DataCamp Power BI course

### Project Overview
This project analyzes the HR performance of a fictitious company, Atlas Lab. The analysis explores hiring trend at Atlas Lab, employee demographics and performance. It also dives deeper into attrition and what factors impact attrition. This analysis will help the organization determine what action they will need to take to retain more employees.

Employee attrition, also known as employee churn or turnover, refers to employees leaving an organization for any reason (voluntary or involuntary).

Using Power BI, I explored employees performance satisfaction by year, employee demographics, and attrition rate.

### Data Sources
The datasets used in this project includes one Fact table 'PerformanceRating.csv' and four Dimension tables: 'EducationLevel.csv', 'Employee.csv', ' Rating level.csv', Satisfied rating.csv' and one date table, 'DimDate.txt'

All datasets were provided by DataCamp

### Tools
Power BI was used to explore the data, DAXs were used to model and transform the data.

### Business Questions
 - Where do the biggest growth in new employees occur?
 - How many active employees do we have?
 - How do we do diversity and inclusion wise?
 - How do our employees perform yearly?

### Exploratory Data Analysis
To answer the business questions, the HR data was analyzed to provide the following insights:

 - Employee Hiring Trends
 - Active Employees by Department
 - Active Employees by Job Role and Department
 - Employees by Age
 - Employees by Marital Status
 - Employees by Gender
 - Employees by Average Salary and Ethnicity
 - Attrition Rate

### Key Findings
 - 2022 saw the highest number of hirings
 - Technology department has more active employees than any other department
 - Of 1470 employees, 1233 are active and 237 are inactive
 - Atlas Lab has an attrition rate of 16.1%
 - Sales Representatives have the highest number of attrition rate
 - Employees who travel frequently also have a high attrition rate

### Recommendations
- To combat the high attrition rate amongst employees with high travel rate, HR should review travel requirement policy and survey employees on feelings around travel frequency.
- There should be sit downs with low performing employees to discuss reasons for low performance and also improvement plans.
